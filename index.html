Short and clear — before you call.

Zemp & Partner Asset Advisory AG is a FINIG-licensed asset manager in Baar. Through the platform we connect you with partner banks for overnight cash, term deposits and — in some regions — simple ETF portfolios. One login instead of an account at every bank.

Bank deposits in the EU are generally covered by statutory deposit protection up to €100,000 per customer and institution. ETF portfolios are segregated assets and move with the market. Rates on the platform are illustrative.

Open an account, sign in, deposit, and open products in the marketplace. Zemp & Partner Asset Advisory AG in Baar (Canton of Zug) remains the operator — imprint and commercial register are below.
